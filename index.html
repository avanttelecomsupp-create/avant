About Avant Telecom

Specialized Engineering, Supply & Telecom Project Management

Our Capabilities

Comprehensive Telecom Services

From site surveys to active deployment and ongoing CAMC, we provide full-spectrum engineering support.

Installation & Commissioning (I&C)

I&C of Microwave, BTS (2G/4G/5G), Small Cells, UBR, and Optical Line Terminals (OLT). Antenna height increment and sector additions.

  • Microwave Link Installation
  • GSM & LTE BTS Setup
  • RET & Sector Additions

Surveys & Site Optimization

P2P/PMP LOS site surveys, RF cluster drive tests, network management, and KPI benchmarking for telecom operators.

  • LOS & P2P/PMP Surveys
  • Pre & Post Drive Testing
  • RF Optimization & OSS Config

CAMC & Managed Services

Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (CAMC), preventive maintenance, corrective breakdown response, and site relocation.

  • 24/7 Breakdown Maintenance
  • Active/Passive Site Audits
  • Out-of-Warranty Support

Optical Fiber & Supply

Supply of CPRI cables, armored duplex patch cords, RF jump cables, DIN/4.3-10 connectors, ACDB/DCDB boxes, and SMPS power units.

  • Single Mode Duplex CPRI
  • RF Jumper Cables (DIN / 4310)
  • Power Modules & SMPS

Repair & Refurbishment

Functional testing, refurbishment, and repair of active GSM, microwave radios, passive infrastructure, and antenna modules.

  • Microwave & Radio Repair
  • Antenna Refurbishment
  • Passive Material Overhaul

TSP Civil & Manpower

Civil works for tower foundations, pole/mast fabrication, customer premises equipment (CPE) deployment, and skilled technical manpower.

  • Civil Work for Towers/Poles
  • CPE & Enterprise Router Cabling
  • Telecom Technical Manpower
